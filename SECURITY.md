# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately by email to **team@binatir.com**. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

Include the affected contract and function, the network and address if deployed, a description of the impact, and steps or a proof of concept to reproduce it. We will acknowledge your report and keep you updated while we investigate and fix it.

## Scope

- `src/OpenHypeCollectible.sol` and its deployed proxies listed in the README.
- Files under `src/test/` are test fixtures and are out of scope.

## Status

The contracts have not been audited yet.
